abstract_en: >-
  This interview was conducted online on 10 November 2025. Professor Liu Huawen is a professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Foreign Languages, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where he leads the translation discipline. His work spans the Hermeneutical Approach in Translation Studies, translation poetics, cognition and translation, interlingual historical writing, and bilingual lexicography. The interview covers his practice as a translator, his teaching philosophy, and his reflections on the interplay between research and practice, as well as the prospects of translation education in the age of AI.
