abstract_en: >-
  Communication in humanity has evolved from a single verbal mode of communication to interaction realized through various modes such as text, image, and sound against the backdrop of constantly shifting new media technologies. The documentary film about the response to COVID-19 has the characteristics of presenting the real world, employing real people and things as content subjects, and communicating meaning through various materials and modalities. Leveraging multimodal discourse analysis to examine the subtitle translation of such medical humanities documentaries can improve the quality and international acceptance of Chinese documentaries on healthcare, as well as the international image and cultural soft power of China's health sector. This study applies literature review and case study. The soundtrack and subtitles of a bilingual documentary, The Lockdown: One Month in Wuhan produced by China International Television (CGTN), are transcribed, annotated, classified, counted and retrieved as a multimodal corpus based on a proposed model, revised from Delabastita's and Qian & Feng's intersemiotic shift models. Quantitative analysis is conducted on the frequency of each subtype of intersemiotic shift strategies, and the descriptive, qualitative analysis of typical cases is performed. The following questions are answered in this study: (1) What are the intersemiotic transcription strategies for the information in the soundtracks to be rendered into subtitles in the documentary on the COVID-19 response? (2) How are the subtitles of the documentary interlingually rendered from the source text into the target text? (3) How do the non-verbal modes affect the strategies for intersemiotic processes in pre-translation editing and interlingual translation? This study found that addition (7%), omission (84%), and compensation (9%) are three major strategies in the pre-translation intersemiotic shift of subtitles in the documentary on COVID-19 response when the auditory modality is rendered to the visual modality, primarily due to the spatial limitation of the visual modality of the subtitles, the irregularity of the spoken language, the specificity of the Wuhan dialect, and the complementary relationship between the multimodal information. In the interlingual shift of the subtitle's source text to the target text, translation strategies such as addition (51%), omission (6%), omi-addition (2%), compensation (19%), and typographic transformation (22%) are adopted, and the main influencing factors are cultural differences, para-verbal information, interpersonal relationships, and multimodal elements such as complementary information of the audio-visual text.
abstract_zh: >-
  在新媒体技术日新月异的背景下，人类从单一的语言模态交际转向文本、图像、声音等多模态实现的交际。抗击新冠疫情纪录片具有描写纪录现实世界，以真人真事作为内容题材、多素材与多模态共同表达意义等特点。从多模态话语分析角度研究此类医学人文类题材纪录片字幕翻译，有助于提高以中国卫生健康为题材的国产纪录片质量和海外接受度，提升中国卫生健康领域的国际形象与文化软实力。本研究采用文献研究法和个案分析法，以中国国际电视台（CGTN）出品的中英双语抗击新冠疫情纪录片《武汉战疫纪》为多模态语料，基于Delabastita和Qian&Feng的符号转换改进模型，对纪录片音轨内容和字幕翻译进行转写、标注、分类、统计、检索，定量统计各符号转换策略的使用频次和频率，对典型案例进行描写性定性分析。拟回答以下问题：（1）在音轨转写到源语过程中，抗击新冠疫情纪录片字幕的译前编辑策略有哪些？（2）在源语转换成译语过程中，抗击新冠疫情纪录片字幕的语际翻译策略有哪些？（3）抗击新冠疫情纪录片中的多模态元素如何影响字幕的译前编辑和翻译策略选择？本研究发现，抗击新冠疫情纪录片中，听觉模态转成视觉模态时，字幕的译前符际转换采取了增补（7%）、省略（84%）和补偿（9%）等策略，主要原因是字幕视觉模态的空间限制、口语的不规范性、武汉方言的特殊性、多模态信息互补关系。字幕源语转换至译语的语际转换过程中，采取了增译（51%）、省译（6%）、省译伴增译（2%）、补偿（19%）、排版转换（22%）等翻译策略，主要影响因素有文化差异、副言语信息、人际关系、音画文的信息互补性等多模态元素。
